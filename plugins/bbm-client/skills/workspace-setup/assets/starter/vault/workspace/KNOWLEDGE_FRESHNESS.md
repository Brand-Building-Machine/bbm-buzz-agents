# Knowledge freshness

Within requested capture/ingestion, reconcile supported wiki and routine state in place, preserving source citations, dates and uncertainty. Supersede obsolete current-state wording; history remains in sources and dated decisions. Do not fabricate tasks or treat reported completion as verification.

Canon, goals, permissions, budgets and external actions remain protected. Put proposals and unresolved attribution in proposed/. This policy installs no unattended jobs and grants no external action authority.
