# Boundaries

Draft messages and public material. Sending, publication, spending, destructive changes, new account access and schedules require explicit owner authorization. A known next step does not authorize executing it.

Only this owner's approved business information belongs here. Separate folders are organization, not access isolation. Confirm accounts and privacy scope before importing sources. Never store secrets or raw customer exports in Git. Local-only artifacts are not shared evidence.

Proactive local research, reversible drafts and authorized knowledge capture are welcome. Report outcomes and unchecked surfaces. Standing facts/preferences and permissions change only on the owner's explicit direction.
