# Urgency and Scarcity — Doctrine

## Principles (rules)

### What they are
- Urgency encourages the reader to decide now rather than later. It comes in two forms: natural urgency (why deciding now beats waiting, and the cost of inaction, which lives mostly in the Close), and deadlines (limited-time enrollment, discounts, or bonuses).
- Scarcity is limited availability: a limited number of spots (5, 50, 100), limited seats in a tier, or a limited-spot bonus ("the first 100 clients get an extra bonus").
- Done genuinely, both can raise conversions substantially.

### Genuine only (the gate)
- Use urgency and scarcity only when they are real. Fake urgency (a countdown timer that simply resets or disappears when it hits zero, or invented scarcity) can raise conversions in the short term, and it is a trust-killer: the moment a client notices, they can lose trust in the brand and never buy again.
- Treat fake urgency as a real problem to flag, not something to credit. A genuine deadline, real limited slots, or a real limited-time bonus is what earns credit. Deadline-funnel software that creates real per-visitor deadlines is a legitimate middle ground.

### How to incorporate it
- Countdown timers, where there is a real deadline (typically at the top of the page, the bottom, and near the buy buttons).
- A limited-time bonus, presented like your other bonuses with a "limited time" headline and clear expiry copy, and reflected in the Buy Buttons bullets.
- Limited-spot copy in the Product Tiers and Buy Buttons sections, ideally with a count that goes down as spots fill.
- Extra urgency or scarcity copy woven into the FAQ, Close, and Offer Introduction, so it is consistent across the page.

## Examples

- **Genuine scarcity (The Lab):** limited spots on the premium tiers ("3 spots available," "1 spot available"). — why it works: real, specific limited slots create genuine scarcity, especially with a count that drops as they fill.

- **A genuine limited-time bonus (AI Dream Team):** "Enroll by February 5th to get 4 live support calls with Natalie... this is the only time we'll offer this live support." — why it works: it is a real, time-boxed offer, presented like the other bonuses with a "limited time" headline and clear expiry copy, on a page whose enrollment is otherwise year-round.

- **A timer, with a caution (The Home Studio Blueprint):** a discount countdown at the top of the page and under each buy button. — why it works, with the caution: the placement of timers is right (top, bottom, near the buttons), but this appears to use a resetting or deadline-funnel timer. Use a real deadline; a fake, resetting one is a trust risk.

## Scoring criteria

1. **The urgency or scarcity is genuine, not fake:** (violation check) Is the urgency or scarcity real (a genuine deadline, real limited slots, or a real limited-time bonus)? MET when it is genuine. PARTIAL when it is real but thin or vague. MISSING when it is fake (a resetting timer, invented scarcity), which is a trust risk to flag rather than credit.
2. **Uses natural urgency:** Does the page use natural urgency (why deciding now beats waiting, and the cost of inaction)? MET when natural urgency is present. PARTIAL when it is faint. MISSING when there is none. (Natural urgency lives mostly in the Close; check that the page has it.)
3. **Scarcity is concrete:** If using scarcity, is it concrete (a specific number of spots, tier seats, or a first-X bonus)? MET when it is specific. PARTIAL when it is vague ("limited spots"). Set aside if not using scarcity. (product-dependent)
4. **Countdown timers in the right places:** If using a deadline, are there countdown timers in the right places (top and bottom of the page, and near the buy buttons)? (visual — audit must ask the user) (product-dependent — set aside if there is no deadline)
5. **Limited-time bonus presented well:** If using a limited-time bonus, is it presented like the other bonuses, with a "limited time" headline and expiry copy, and reflected in the Buy Buttons bullets? MET when it is. PARTIAL when the expiry copy or the bullet update is missing. Set aside if there is no limited-time bonus. (product-dependent)
6. **Woven consistently into the page:** Is the urgency or scarcity woven consistently into the other sections (FAQ, Close, Offer Introduction)? MET when it is consistent across the page. PARTIAL when it appears in only one spot. Set aside if there is no deadline or scarcity to weave. (product-dependent)
