# Skill: section-audit (one part of any piece)

*Amended 2026-11-04, v2 batch 1: one part of any piece, mapped through copy-types.md (V2-2, V2-3), the deliberate-choices step removed (V2-23), paths to this package's resources (V2-22).*

Invoke: "run audit on [section]" — then I'll paste or point to the copy to audit.

## Steps

1. Read the doctrine file(s) `copy-types.md` maps this part to (for a sales-page section, `resources/doctrine/[section].md`) (its Scoring criteria + Principles + Examples).
2. Read `resources/lean-editing.md` (the universal craft layer). Read this on EVERY audit, whatever the section. Its 8 criteria apply on top of the section's own criteria. Also read `resources/coach-voice.md` and follow it for the voice, tone, and word choice of everything you write in the audit. Apply its never-say rules to your OWN wording (the report prose and the scorecard), not just to the copy you are judging. Before sending, reread your draft against that never-say list and rewrite any line that breaks it.
3. Read the copy I give you.

Then follow `resources/audit.md` for all judging, scoring, and report format.
