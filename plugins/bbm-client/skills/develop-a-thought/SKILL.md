Resolve the selected workspace using `bbm-client.json` or explicit `BBM_CLIENT_CONFIG`, following [workspace-start](../workspace-start/SKILL.md). Read its map and boundaries before saving. Resolve paths from that config, never from the plugin cache or another workspace. If no workspace is configured, conversation-only work can continue; use workspace-setup before filing. Preserve existing files and keep private information in its selected scope.

## Conversation

- Let the person supply material in their own order and format. Reflect the central idea briefly as your reading, not an approved position.
- Follow a meaningful unfinished thread with one open question at a time when helpful. Ask about meaning, tension, or scope before seeking scene details for a future script. Do not impose a questionnaire, required number of questions, or fixed number of rounds.
- A good question follows something they actually said. For example, “Can you stay with that part a little longer?” can be more useful than demanding a date, a turning point, and an exact quotation.
- Do not require an answer to every gap before saving. Stop probing when the present purpose is met; preserve an unfinished thought as unfinished.
- Keep your messages short unless the person asks for depth. Do not narrate every filing operation or give them a long list of topics gained.

## Reflect and confirm

Offer a short synthesis when it would help check understanding or serve as a reusable representation. Keep it distinct from verbatim words. Preserve causal qualifications, uncertainty, and the difference between a remembered experience and a general claim. Do not strengthen “can” to “will,” infer a sole cause, or add an exclusion the speaker never made.

Record a clear confirmation against the exact synthesis version and the confirming message. A correction creates a revised version; ask again only if it materially changes what will represent their view. Do not make the person approve each verbatim contribution. “Yes, and…” confirms the identifiable prior synthesis and adds new source material; it does not automatically approve your subsequent rewrite combining them.

Research can help explore a live question. Use appropriate available research tools, cite sources, and distinguish an author's statement, Scripture, interpretation, and the person's agreement. Respect for an author does not imply agreement with every claim.

## Capture

When filing is requested or already authorized, save a source-linked note in the configured STATE_PATH, using the workspace map. Preserve the person's actual words separately from the synthesis, the prompt that elicited them, confirmation status and unresolved questions. An unfinished thought stays unfinished. Confirmation of one synthesis does not approve a later materially changed version.

Do not promote an exploratory thought into standing preferences or canon, or imply permission to use it publicly. Content production and publication are separate requests. This version is self-contained; no worldview-curation plugin is required.
