---
name: humanizer
description: "Edit AI-sounding prose into natural writing while preserving meaning, factual claims and the writer's voice."
---

Edit the supplied text for its intended reader. Use an existing voice sample when provided; do not invent personality, anecdotes or proof.

Look for clusters of problems rather than treating one phrase as proof of AI authorship:
- Inflated significance, vague praise and unsupported claims about trends or consensus.
- Stock contrasts, repetitive triads, staged questions and answers, canned openings and closers.
- Excessive headings, bolding, em dashes and one-line fragments that obscure the argument.
- Abstract nouns and ornate verbs where concrete subjects and actions would be clearer.
- Chatbot residue, explanations of the editing process and background the reader already knows.

Lead with the point. Replace vague language with supplied specifics, or remove the unsupported claim. Vary rhythm naturally without manufacturing quirks. Preserve intentional wording, quotations, names, technical terms, uncertainty and qualifications. Do not turn a cautious claim into certainty, change numbers, erase meaningful caveats or rewrite a quotation as if verbatim.

Read the revision as a whole for coherence and voice. Return the edited text; briefly explain material meaning questions only when needed. Do not publish or send it. If saving is requested, follow workspace-start and the workspace's draft destination.
