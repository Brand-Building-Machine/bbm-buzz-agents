# Meta Ads Desk

The Meta skills (`meta-audit`, `meta-creative`, `meta-launch`, `meta-tracking`) and the Meta Ads Desk persona adapt method from the projects below. The text and code here are rewritten, not copied; the method is theirs.

- **creative-strategy-skills** by Motion (Alysha, Creative Strategy Lead), https://github.com/motion-team/creative-strategy-skills (MIT): the customer-language, pain/persona/angle, awareness, hook and mechanic method in `meta-creative`.
- **marketingskills** by Corey Haines, https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills (MIT): the TCPL-anchored kill/keep/scale rules, ad-count ceiling, fatigue bands and hook system. Its decision system credits Ivan Falco's practitioner framework; we adapted only the MIT-licensed text above.
- **meta-ads-analyzer** by Mathias Chu, https://github.com/mathiaschu/meta-ads-analyzer (MIT): the breakdown-effect, learning-phase and fluctuation rules in `meta-audit`.
- **marketing-skills** by hyperfx.ai, https://github.com/hyperfx-ai/marketing-skills (MIT): Meta Marketing API gotchas for lead campaigns in `meta-launch`.
- **paid-media-agent** by LangChain, https://github.com/langchain-ai/paid-media-agent (Apache License 2.0): the propose / approve / apply / read-back change flow and analysis rules. License: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
