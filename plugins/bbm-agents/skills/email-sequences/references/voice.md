# The owner's email voice (email-voice.md)

Emails go out under the owner's name, so they have to sound like the owner. The brand folder's `voice-agent.md` covers the brand's voice in general; `email-voice.md` (same folder) covers how this owner actually writes email. Every email skill reads both. `emailkit.py voice init` creates the file from a template; it never overwrites.

## Build it from evidence, once

Best source by far: **10-20 emails the owner actually sent** to clients and prospects (not newsletters). Ask them to paste them or drop them into the workspace as text. If a mail connector is available, only read the owner's sent mail with their yes, and only their own mailbox.

Weaker sources: their website, LinkedIn posts, a voice note. If there are no samples at all, ask for three emails they were happy with. Do not guess a voice.

From the samples, write down what someone could copy:

- Greeting habit ("Hey Sam," / "Hi Sam," / no greeting, straight in)
- Sign-off and name exactly as they write it
- Sentence length, contractions, how direct the ask is
- Words and phrases they use; words they never use
- Exclamation marks and emoji: ever, rarely, never
- How they talk about their own service (what they call it)
- How formal they get with a new lead versus a long-time client

Then show the owner a two-line sample in that voice and ask "does this sound like you?" Adjust until it does.

## Sending profile (same file)

Facts the emails need, asked once:

- Sender name and sign-off
- Booking link (calendar) and main reply address
- Email platform, if any (Gmail/Outlook only, or a tool like Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, Kit, HubSpot, GoHighLevel)
- Plain text or designed for marketing emails
- Business postal address (marketing emails must include one to comply with anti-spam laws like CAN-SPAM)
- Anything they never say or promise (regulated claims, pricing they don't publish)

## Keep it learning

When the owner edits a draft, note the pattern (not the one-off) under **Learned preferences** with the date: "2026-10-02: never says 'hop on a call', says 'grab 15 minutes'." A correction made once should never need making twice.
