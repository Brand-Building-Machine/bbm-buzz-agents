# Post templates

Pick by the search. Lengths are planning ranges for a small business, not targets: stop when the reader's task is done.

| The search looks like | Template | Typical length |
|---|---|---|
| "how to ...", "how do I ..." | How-to | 1,000-1,800 |
| "what is ...", "... explained", a question | Explainer / Q&A | 800-1,500 |
| "best ...", "top ...", "... ideas", "... mistakes" | List | 1,000-1,800 |
| "X vs Y", "X or Y", "... alternatives" | Comparison | 1,200-2,000 |
| the broad topic behind a whole cluster | Pillar guide | 2,000-3,500 |
| a real client result | Case study | 700-1,200 |
| a rule, limit or law just changed | What changed | 700-1,200 |

## How-to

Intro (the goal and who it's for, what they need first) / Key takeaways / "Before you start" (requirements, time, cost) / numbered steps, each an H2 or H3 starting with a verb, each with the why and the common mistake / "If something goes wrong" / when to call a pro (natural CTA) / author bio.

## Explainer / Q&A

Answer the question in the first 40-60 words with a plain definition ("X is..."). Key takeaways / how it works / who it applies to / what it costs or what it means for the reader / common misconceptions (from the brand bible) / related questions as H2s / CTA / author bio.

## List

Intro that says how the list was chosen / key takeaways / numbered H2 items, each with what it is, why it matters, and one specific example / a short "how to choose" section / CTA after item 3-4 and at the end.

## Comparison

The verdict in the first paragraph ("For most small businesses, X, unless...") / comparison table with a header row (cost, who it suits, pros, cons) / one H2 per option / "which one is right for you" by situation / CTA after the recommendation. Fair to the option the owner doesn't sell.

## Pillar guide

What this guide covers and for whom / key takeaways / a table of contents / one H2 per sub-topic, each a solid summary that links to the spoke post that goes deep / glossary if the field has jargon / CTA at 2-3 natural breaks. Links to every spoke in the cluster and to the service page.

## Case study

The result first, with the number / the client's situation (anonymized unless they approved naming) / the problem / what was done, step by step / the result with figures and timeframe / 3-5 takeaways for a reader in the same spot / CTA to the related service. Real cases only, with the client's permission.

## What changed

What changed and when, in the first paragraph, with the official source / who it affects / what to do now (numbered) / what hasn't changed / the deadline / CTA. Link the primary source (the regulator, the agency, the law).

## CTA placement

How-to: after the steps. List: after item 3-4 and at the end. Comparison: after the recommendation. Case study: after the results. Pillar: at 2-3 section breaks. Benefit-led, specific to the post, in the owner's voice. One main goal per post.
